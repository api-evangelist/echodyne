# Echodyne

> Echodyne is a radar platform company that designs and manufactures advanced MESA® radar systems for defense, government, critical infrastructure protection, uncrewed aircraft systems (UAS), advanced air mobility (AAM), and autonomous platforms.

Echodyne combines patented metamaterials electronically scanned array (MESA®) technology with powerful software and analytics to deliver high‑fidelity situational awareness in compact, commercially exportable radar systems.
